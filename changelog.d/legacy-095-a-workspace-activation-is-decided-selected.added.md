**A workspace activation is decided, selected and served by one owner.** `WorkspaceActivationReservation::decide` takes `Gate(Env)` once and answers `Attach` or reserves exactly the next environment generation from the frozen publish inputs, so two callers cannot revoke a matching live session or create two activations; a changed policy, profile, backend or artifact supersedes with a fenced successor, while a target that disagrees with the published container or boot, or a generation whose selection is unfinished, refuses by name. The workspace commit now records the guest selection it published — binary, context and credential objects, the three expected→next selector values and the serving lease — in the same transaction as `active(E)`, the session mapping and the session's `Borrow` attachment. That is the only commit: the generic one refuses rather than publishing a generation with no durable selection, a generation never *decided* refuses as unfrozen, the artifact, target and context selection must be the ones the decision froze (a fresh project's unselected artifact is frozen by its first publication), the context document is parsed and checked field by field, and the commit requires the session's own live launch — bound, qualified and rooted in the run that owns it. `unfinished_selection`, `Attachments::selection_switched`, `selection_recovered` (three proven liveness facts *and* every selector and object read back through a `SelectorReader` under `Gate(Env)` at its committed value) and `selection_abandoned` (only once the exact successor is settled) complete, complete-from-recovery or revoke it without ever publishing an unserved generation or restoring the predecessor. Concurrent decision callers are proven to reserve one generation or attach one session. One session owns its listener and memory-only serving key (`SessionLaunch`/`ChannelBound`/`ServingLease`) for the session's durable lifetime, and refuses to publish an unbound, unqualified or stopped listener. Cached locators are revalidated, and a selection stays Pending if its successor loses owner or listener custody before live or recovery completion. Selector reads are bound to the committed container/boot, prior values are observed before receipt construction, and deserialized receipts cannot publish. Transport, TLS qualification and the runner/container adapters remain with #482/#483/#452.
