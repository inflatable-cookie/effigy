Bind the fixed Parallels host-inventory reader to its boot handoff (effigy#782). The helper bytes are pinned by a committed literal in `linux_disk_association`, not by a digest the helper computes from itself, so modified helper bytes refuse. `read_inventory` takes the stage's already authenticated boot identity and a guest boot-original reader, calls it once immediately before and once immediately after the one bounded dispatch, and derives the selected VM UUID and home from the helper's own output; a changed or malformed bracket, a nonzero or unwaited exit, a timeout, a resource or output-cap refusal, an over-cap, partial or non-ASCII read, a malformed read and another VM all refuse rather than becoming a host record. The diagnostic observation stage now assembles the complete association record inside its same-VM boot bracket and feeds it through production `setup.verify_disk_association`. The outcome stores that fact document (raw config, descriptor and host-read stdout/stderr as bounded base64 envelopes with length and digest), because `setup_plan` E7* re-verifies that key as facts; the reduced verify result is kept beside it and is never the stored record. Controls use fixture transport; no VM or guest effect.
