**A committed workspace session's service outlives the call that selected it.** `workspace_client::supervisor` binds the container-up handoff to a durable owner: `ServingHandoff` accepts only the committed selected session (the launch must have published exactly the lease its `SelectionReceipt` froze, for that session, with its key still in custody) and moves the adopted supervisor, prepared listener, validated facts and admitted clients into `ServiceOwner`, which keeps them across the selecting call's return and serves until the session stops, its owner is lost or its custody refuses. `ServiceOwner::retire` stops the listener, wipes the key and settles the session through its own close transaction exactly once; an interrupted handoff closes its prepared candidate and reports it instead of claiming custody, and `discover` names the environment's session, its durable phase and any unfinished commit's lease from the checkout journal alone, so a recovery or down driver in another command finds the same session with no process-local registry.
