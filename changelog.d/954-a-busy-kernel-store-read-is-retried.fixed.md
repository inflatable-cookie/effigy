A read of the kernel store now retries a store another process is writing instead of reporting the journal as unavailable. The read's open mode comes from the store's own side files, and a supervisor writes the same checkout journal beside every status, log and teardown read, so one attempt could meet SQLite's busy condition on files that changed underneath it and a `container status`, `logs` or `down` read could answer "host journal read is unavailable" under load. Each attempt now re-reads those side files, inside the caller's own remaining observation window and for at most five seconds, and a store that stays held for the whole window is still reported as busy rather than retried forever. No store is created, repaired or locked for writing, and the bounded read never waits on the store's writer gate (effigy#954).
