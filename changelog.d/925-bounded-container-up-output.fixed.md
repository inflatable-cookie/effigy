`effigy container up` now shows a runtime's own output as a bounded rolling window of its latest lines under the stage label instead of printing the whole stream, and the window collapses into the stage's timed result. The complete stream stays in the run's retained backend log, whose path is printed once; a failure prints the last ~30 lines and that path before one failure sentence. nerdctl's known unsupported-Compose-field warnings (`Ignoring: … [Labels]`, `[Driver Labels]`, `depends_on … [Required]`) are counted into one summary line rather than echoed. Piped, CI, `--json` and managed-pane output keep their existing behaviour and no JSON field changes.
