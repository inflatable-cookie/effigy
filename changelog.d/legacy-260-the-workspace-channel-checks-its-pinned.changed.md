**The workspace channel checks its pinned host identity before it speaks.** A guest client compares the server identity the context pinned against the identity its transport verified before writing a single byte, so a mispinned peer receives no hello, version, digest or capability. The v0.15 policy requires a connection that dies before answering to report `Unknown` without resending or running locally; full policy enforcement remains in progress. An attachment may reconnect at its consumed cursor, and a gap never advances it.
