A capture pump drain commits at most one bounded 64 KiB window per stream instead of one durable append per pipe read. A chatty producer's full pipe paid the append-and-provenance dance (and the styled-tap window rewrite) up to sixteen times per drain, so the canonical full-pipe capture fixture could exceed its committed-extent observation bound under shared I/O load and fail intermittently (effigy#558, effigy#548). Arrival order, redaction, the per-turn read bound, prompt stop and capture identity are unchanged; the burst the fixture observes now commits in about a sixth of the time.
