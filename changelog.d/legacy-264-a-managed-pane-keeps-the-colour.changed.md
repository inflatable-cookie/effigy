**A managed pane keeps the colour its captured child would have drawn, and its captured byte stream stays plain.** A nested Cargo or Oxide producer whose output Effigy captures through its custody writer receives the initiating pane's ANSI capability and keeps its colour in the pane, while that captured log and every `--json`, doctor, status and error reader keep sanitized plain text and never interpret terminal controls. `NO_COLOR`, CI and an explicit colour preference still win, and a pane without ANSI support, or one whose style window expired, renders plain text. This is a statement about the captured stream only: output a child writes through a descriptor it inherited, instead of through that writer, is outside it, and securing that direct log path is open lead `a52a71e5`, not a v0.15 claim.
