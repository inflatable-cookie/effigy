`effigy doctor --deep` runs the `health` task while the shared repository walk runs, instead of after it, so a deep scan on a large repository takes about the longer of the two rather than their sum. Findings, check order and the JSON contract are unchanged. If the walk runs out of budget, a health task that finished keeps its findings and reports `complete`, and one the deadline cut off reports `budget-exhausted`; previously the health task was always reported as skipped.
