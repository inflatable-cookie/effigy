A probe's deadline now bounds the workload it released rather than the observer's own fork, spawn barrier and registration handshake, so a delayed fork on a busy host no longer turns a bounded probe into an early "timed out during spawn barrier" refusal. The kernel fault harness also measures a faulted child's progress instead of its wall time and follows a guarded probe's exit instead of judging one sampled instant; the scenario points, observations, oracles, deadlines and grace periods are unchanged.
