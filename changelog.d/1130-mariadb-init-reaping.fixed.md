The MariaDB catalog service now runs under an init process (`init: true`) on both Docker and Colima `nerdctl` backends, so the children its periodic health check leaves behind are reaped instead of accumulating as zombies under `mariadbd`. The health check and graceful shutdown are unchanged. The change alters the render of every MariaDB stack, so the next `container up` recreates each MariaDB container once and keeps its data volume.
