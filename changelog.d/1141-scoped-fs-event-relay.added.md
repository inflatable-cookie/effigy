Added a scoped host-to-guest file-event relay, configured by `[containers.<name>.host].fs_events` (`auto`, `on` or `off`, with optional `paths`, `ignore`, `debounce_ms` and `services`): under `auto` it runs for the life of an attached `dev`, foreground `container up`, interactive `container shell` or `workspace` session, explicit `on` also keeps one supervised relay with a detached stack until it goes down, `container status` and `doctor` report its state, and `doctor` warns while a Colima profile's `mountInotify: true` relays the same events.
