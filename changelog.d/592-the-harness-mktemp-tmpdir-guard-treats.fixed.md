The harness `mktemp` TMPDIR guard treats an unescaped `$(mktemp …)` or backtick substitution inside double quotes as a command again, while still ignoring quoted mentions, single-quoted literals, and escaped dollar-paren or backtick openers (effigy#592). `effigy test:qa:static-guards` names those cases; `qa:docs:mktemp` stays the live-tree check.
