Wall-clock-bounded proof fixtures no longer race the host's scheduling, so a loaded machine reports the behaviour they assert instead of a spent budget. The workspace-integration orchestrator's budget checks read a clock the adapter supplies (production keeps the monotonic clock) and its fake adapters advance that clock themselves, and the detached runtime-integration success proofs use the declared health-timeout default rather than a two-second activation budget. The nested remote-deadline proof takes its inherited budget where production hands the workload's environment over and installs the model session's exit trap before it registers. The detached-capture inventory proof holds its caller's deadline at a frozen reading until the workload publishes its own identity, then spends the same deadline. Kernel custody fixtures mint one cleanup clock per phase instead of sharing one across a run's whole life, express the escalation bound relative to the fixture's ten-second grace, and keep their poll bounds as one generous hang guard rather than per-wait deadlines. The checks, their assertions and every real timeout are unchanged (effigy#957).
