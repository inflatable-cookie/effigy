The mounts-budget estimate now models an option-less primary-service `tmpfs` list as writable tmpfs rows with an empty source beside the merged volume mounts, counting their bytes against the same guarded budget. Effigy's own php-fpm catalog renders a `/run/effigy/secrets` tmpfs on the primary service, and the previous refusal of the whole `tmpfs` field made every such stack inadmissible on the Colima nerdctl fallback before seed, data, status or any up could run. A `tmpfs` mapping form or an option-carrying entry (`/path:size=…`) still refuses as an unmodelled serialization, with paths omitted (effigy#546).
