An environment transition settles on a budget that starts when settlement does. The cleanup context used to be made when the transition opened, so a cold stack whose activation ran past 60 seconds left its settlement already expired and the command failed with `environment record ... timed out ... waiting for the environment to settle` after an apply that had succeeded. Each settlement now takes a fresh bounded 60-second budget: admission makes its own once `Gate(Env)` is held, so a transition that queued behind another one settles an interrupted predecessor on a whole budget, and the run's own settle and every settling holder make theirs when they start. `container up` and `container down` show the gate wait and the settlement as steps.
