Human failures name their cause in words instead of printing a Rust `Debug` tree, across the container, release, managed-run and bundle-source planes: an unreadable kernel record reports the store's own sentence and next action, a failed container command reports `exit code 1` rather than `Some(1)`, a release refusal lists its unsettled effects as `publish tag` is `unknown`, and a manifest's unparsable source lock names what could not be read. A stop that interrupts a managed release now reads as the cancellation it is, instead of reporting that the evidence contradicts itself. The human-error Debug ratchet now covers the container policy, manifest and managed-run vocabularies that the Q-017 audit found outside it, and its baseline dropped to 102 specifiers from 135.
