`EFFIGY_STRICT_PERSISTENCE=1` opts a process into a run that prepares nothing: no store creation, schema `ALTER`/backfill, expired-row prune or checkpoint (at `COMMIT` or at the last connection's close), and no maintenance either — checkpoint, vacuum, retention pass, machine compaction and the explicit repair all do nothing or refuse under the switch. A journal that is not already prepared, or that needs recovery (a rollback journal, a torn log, or a log whose wal-index is missing), is refused **before the admission hook runs**, with a typed refusal naming `effigy run prepare` — the new step that prepares a checkout journal outside a strict run, with `--check` to verify that nothing would be written. Contract 049 now states the complete strict contribution with its arithmetic: `32 + 57 × (page size + 24)` bytes of write-ahead log where the 57 pages are the insert's three b-trees (the table and its two indexes, five levels each) plus page 1, the freelist and the auto-vacuum pointer map; 0 bytes of main database; one 32 KiB wal-index block; and no remaining premise about other writers' frames, because a log with frames and no index is now refused. Behaviour without the switch is unchanged (effigy#981).
