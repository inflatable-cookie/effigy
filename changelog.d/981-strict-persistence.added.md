`EFFIGY_STRICT_PERSISTENCE=1` opts a process into a run that prepares nothing: no store creation, schema `ALTER`/backfill, expired-row prune or checkpoint (at `COMMIT` or at the last connection's close), and no maintenance either — checkpoint, vacuum, retention pass, machine compaction and the explicit repair all refuse or do nothing under the switch, and even observing the write-ahead log no longer checkpoints it. A journal that is not already prepared, or that needs recovery (a rollback journal, a torn log, or a log whose wal-index is missing), is refused **before the admission hook runs**, with a typed refusal naming `effigy run prepare` — the new step that prepares a checkout journal outside a strict run, with `--check` to verify that nothing would be written — and a refused open leaves the store's bytes untouched. Contract 049 now states the strict route's complete physical contribution with file:line: 0 bytes of main database, at most 64 KiB of wal-index, one row of at most 960 stored bytes, and a write-ahead-log maximum of 17,316,392 bytes (4203 frames) derived from SQLite's balance and pointer-map behaviour, with the premises that maximum needs enforced by the route itself — a 24 MiB page cache so no spill re-appends a page, and a 64-byte index-key bound in the store it writes. Behaviour without the switch is unchanged (effigy#981).
