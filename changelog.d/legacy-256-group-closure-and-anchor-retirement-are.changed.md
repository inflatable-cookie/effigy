**Group closure and anchor retirement are honest about their limits.** An anchor retires only when one inventory round positively accounts for every in-group row (a zombie cannot fork) and positively observes the anchor itself. A listed row unreadable at its state or identity read, a failed read, or an inventory without the anchor's own row keeps it alive. An unresolved row reports `unproven group closure`. Group closure observed through an inventory follows the same rule. Group signals keep their authority rules unchanged: a verified anchor (member by member), a leader whose live identity is verified immediately before the signal, or an owner that holds its unreaped child; on macOS and Linux before 6.9 the numeric check-to-signal window remains and is documented rather than papered over, so Effigy makes no absolute no-recycled-PGID guarantee there (leads `0453e055`, `b013f65a`).
