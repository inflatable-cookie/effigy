The recovery test fixtures now live in the run's temporary directory (or the confined worker's `EFFIGY_RECOVERY_FIXTURE_PARENT`) instead of falling back to `HOME`, so a lost volume no longer strands unmanaged fixture scratch and its children under the operator's home; the Colima path-guard proof keeps both arms and names the cause when the run's own root is one of the fixed OS temp roots it refuses. A failing kernel-custody proof now prints the process identities its fixture home still records — each workload PID it published and the run's owner, supervisor and sentinel, with kernel start identity and process group — before that home is removed, so leftover processes can be attributed and stopped by recorded pid. The adapterless double-fork gateway fixture, the failing-path identity receipt and the lone-pid controls are unchanged in behaviour; they are proven again with the `gateway_private` listener and kernel custody selectors.
