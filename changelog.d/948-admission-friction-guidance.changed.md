The repository's validation instruction now names the checkout binary by its absolute path (`"$(pwd)/target/debug/effigy"`): a host admission hook that reads the admitted image from the process argv resolves a relative `./target/...` against its own working directory, not the request's, and refuses the run as an unidentifiable workload. The admission guide records that contract — the request's `cwd` and `root` are the paths a hook resolves an image against, and Effigy's own wait for the hook stays bounded by `EFFIGY_ADMISSION_HOOK_TIMEOUT_MS` — and the troubleshooting guide maps the `oxide: waiting for the admission lock` notice to its Oxide pool owner, the selector step's finite lifetime that ends the wait, and the read-only `oxide status` diagnosis, without pool mutation or a plain-Cargo escape (effigy#948).
