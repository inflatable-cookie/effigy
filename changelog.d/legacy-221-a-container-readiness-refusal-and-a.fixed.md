A container readiness refusal and a Colima profile refusal name the failed service or profile, the cause they observed and one `next:` command. A bring-up that never becomes ready ends its diagnostic with a single `next:` line (`effigy container logs --service <NAME>`, then `effigy container up`), and a failed health read points at `effigy doctor`. A failed Colima command names its profile and carries its output as evidence. `--json` carries the same action in `error.human.next_action`.
