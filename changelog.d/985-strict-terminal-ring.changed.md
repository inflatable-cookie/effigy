In strict mode (`EFFIGY_STRICT_PERSISTENCE=1`) a plain foreground admitted task's terminal record now goes to one slot of a fixed-size, preallocated 16 KiB ring (`<checkout>/.effigy/state/foreground-terminals.ring`, 16 slots of 1 KiB) instead of a SQLite row: `effigy run prepare` creates and fully preallocates it outside the route, the route opens no SQLite connection at all and rewrites exactly one 1 KiB slot with no growth or allocation, and a missing, wrong-size, full or corrupt ring refuses before admission rather than falling back to SQLite. A slot is written body-then-commit-marker with an `fsync` between, so a torn write is never read as a record; a repeated settle writes nothing; and the next non-strict store open ingests committed slots into `foreground_terminals`, preserving identity, idempotence, retention and history while `run status` and `runs` read the slot until then. The former strict-mode SQLite terminal-write WAL maximum is gone. Non-strict behaviour is unchanged (effigy#985).
