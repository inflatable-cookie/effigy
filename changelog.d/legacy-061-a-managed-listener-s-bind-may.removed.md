**Breaking:** **A managed listener's `bind` may ask for a port, and keeps the one it gets.** A declared port such as `bind = "127.0.0.1:4173"` is used untouched. A `bind` whose port is `0` asks Effigy for one: before any child launches, Effigy takes the address the process last published or asks the OS for a free loopback port, hands that address to the child as `EFFIGY_MANAGED_HOST_LISTENER_BIND`, `HOST` and `PORT`, verifies it answers the readiness request, and publishes the route on it. A restart or a re-`up` keeps that published address, so a dependent's `internal_url` stays valid; there is no port range, no endpoint store reservation and no port registry behind it.
