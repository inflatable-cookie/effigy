The remaining wall-clock-bounded proof fixtures no longer let a loaded host decide their outcome, so a candidate QA run reports the behaviour each test asserts instead of a spent budget. The served-session enrolment read polls a non-blocking socket until its answer arrives under the guest's existing ten-second guard rather than failing on the first `WouldBlock` or a 400ms tail. The reserved-creator binding proof expires its publication deadline by setting it after the fixture exists instead of waiting inside a two-second window that the fixture itself had to fit. The parked-workspace abort proof parks against the deadline under test while the fixture runs under live authority, and spends its parent authority in a separate short window. The kernel HTTP probe and its integration counterpart scale their fixture request budgets with measured host load instead of a flat 200/250ms. The process-supervisor proofs wait for the event they assert under a load-scaled guard instead of spending a fixed number of short receive slices, and the kernel, container and gateway fixtures that wait on another thread or a peer now use the same load-scaled floor. The named checks, their assertions and every product timeout are unchanged (effigy#994).
