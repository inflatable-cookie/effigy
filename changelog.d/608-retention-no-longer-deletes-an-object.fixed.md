Retention no longer deletes an object a concurrent publication has just made desired or last-applied (effigy#608). A sweep selected its orphan candidates without the objects gate, then removed each selected digest's canonical bytes and row under the gate without rechecking it, so a publication that journalled its intent before the gate and committed its desired reference under it could lose exactly the bytes its references name, leaving `object_refs` and `resources.desired` pointing at a missing object. Retention now rechecks every candidate against the same unreferenced predicate under the gate and skips a digest that gained an intent or a reference between the selection and the delete, so either ordering of the race keeps bytes, row and references coherent. Genuine orphans are still reclaimed, the recheck is one read per candidate — its reference half is a digest lookup and its staging-event half scans the journal, the shape the selection already pays — and the per-candidate writer batches stay as short as before.
