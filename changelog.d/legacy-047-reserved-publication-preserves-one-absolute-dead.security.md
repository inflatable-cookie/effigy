Reserved publication preserves one absolute deadline across planning, backend and profile reads, manifest/source closure, artifact authentication, probes and guest staging. Blocking local reads use bounded workers; expiry is Unknown or a timeout refusal. The post-apply builder checks full container/boot identity, complete backend/profile, image platform and loader, artifact receipt and digest, and immutable source pins before a selector switch or credential. Unknown aliases, raced identities, platform disagreement, tampering and unverified emulation refuse. The probe is owned by the input; the enrolment secret is 32 OS-random bytes rendered as canonical 64-hex. The creator receipt does not establish guest-session publication or native backend qualification.
