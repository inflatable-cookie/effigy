A container runtime that stops answering an inventory read can no longer hold one `container up` open for minutes. Every runtime read Effigy makes is bounded, and a bring-up's read after Compose returns and its readiness wait share one deadline: the environment's declared `[containers.<NAME>.health].timeout_secs` (60s by default), measured from the moment Compose returned. A hung read therefore spends that budget once, its probe child is stopped and reaped at the deadline, and the activation ends with one refusal carrying the usual `state: unavailable`, `readiness: unverified` evidence instead of a second Compose activation.
