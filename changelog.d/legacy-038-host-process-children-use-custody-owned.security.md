Host-process children use custody-owned output pipes instead of inheriting raw log descriptors. Their merged output is sanitized and redacted before append to the existing plain follower path; failed appends stop capture fanout rather than acknowledge unwritten output. Schema-sensitive and unclassified application-specific inherited environment values are passed to custody as secrets. At the first proven stopped boundary, an untrusted legacy host log is retained in private quarantine and replaced at the same path with a fresh canonical inode and durable provenance. Stop and reopen pre-upgrade followers after upgrading; already-open descriptors cannot be revoked. The bundled Underlay follower resets on inode change even when the replacement is larger than its previous byte offset. Effigy readers honor a durable committed extent and hide failed or torn appends; a poisoned log refuses restart for explicit operator recovery. Direct file/Underlay presentation reads may show a safe line before sync that is later lost on crash; they do not acknowledge durability.
