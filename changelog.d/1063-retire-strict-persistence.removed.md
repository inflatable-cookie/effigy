Strict persistence and the reported admission protocol are retired before their first release. `EFFIGY_STRICT_PERSISTENCE`, `effigy run prepare` and `EFFIGY_ADMISSION_PROTOCOL` are refused with one message that names the replacement: runs need no preparation step, and there are no terminal ring and no hook acknowledgements. The admission hook contract is unchanged: `EFFIGY_ADMISSION_HOOK`, the `effigy.admission-request.v1` request and the `admit` or `deny <reason>` answer.
