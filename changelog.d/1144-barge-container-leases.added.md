On a machine whose Barge account declares a Colima profile, `effigy container up`, task-started stacks and the `effigy-release` builder now reserve a container lease before Compose runs, label their containers `barge.lease=<id>`, and wait for a worker slot when the profile is full; every teardown Effigy performs (`down`, `retire`, `reset`, a failed bring-up and the idle stop) returns the lease at once, while a crashed holder's stack is reclaimed by the sweep or `effigy container prune`. Without Barge, or on an undeclared profile, behaviour is unchanged.
