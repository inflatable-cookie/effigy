A failed step now records the output it produced even when that output was relayed live instead of captured, so `effigy qa` and `effigy test -- --no-fail-fast` failures keep their real stream, and the failure summary names the first failing and panic/assertion lines. The record is written to the configured scratch root's `effigy/failed-steps/<run id>/`, which outlives a harness teardown of the run's temporary directory, and the existing bounded sweep retires it after 24 hours.
