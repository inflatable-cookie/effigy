A managed listener whose route republication failed retries while its port keeps answering, instead of staying unreachable until the child next goes silent. `container <env> restart` holds `Gate(Env)` for its stop and spawn only, so a hung readiness wait still yields to `down`, and a failed detach rollback settles only the restarted process, never an unrelated host process. The private orphan-certificate proof now waits on the actual `certs/` pair, and `test:gateway:endpoints` selects the fresh-store watcher proof.
